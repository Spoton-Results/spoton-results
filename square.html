Keep one customer-facing price, or offer different cash and card prices. Here's the difference.

Pay processing fees as you go

Clear answers before you get started.

Does Square Free mean payments are free?

No. There is no monthly software subscription fee for Square Free, but processing fees apply. Hardware and optional services can cost extra.

Does dual pricing eliminate every fee?

No. The cash/card price difference can help offset processing costs, but processing on the final card amount and other charges affect the result. Software and equipment may have separate costs.

Do I have to call before signing up?

No. Use the signup button for the pricing approach you prefer. Call if you want help choosing or need your support arrangement explained before enrollment.

Can I use this link if I already have Square?

Contact us first to check eligibility. Do not create a duplicate account just to use a signup link.

Who provides support?

SpotOn Results is your contact for discussing this offer. Turnkey provides support according to the applicable arrangement, while Square supplies its platform and plan-based support. Confirm your support contacts and hours before enrollment.
